Design Concept Evaluation

Three bespoke architectural directions were created. Concept 01 was selected as the timeless winner.
Selected Winner Concept

Concept 01: The Architectural Pillar & Keystone Monogram

Integrates an authentic Doric fluted stone column on the left forming the vertical stem of 'B', intertwined with precision architectural arches and a chiseled keystone chevron forming the 'M'. Grounded by monumental stone plinths and diamond cut pins.

Verdict: Highest architectural heritage, luxury balance, and flawless scalability across CNC stone inlays, laser engraving, and digital screens.

Concept 02: Geometric Bookmatched Marble Slabs

Features 45-degree isometric beveled stone slabs forming a modern diamond B/M monogram. While ultra-contemporary, it leans toward industrial tile fabrication rather than artisanal luxury stonemasonry.

Concept 03: Monumental Roman Triumphal Archway

Encompasses a grand classical vault framing intertwined calligraphic serif letterforms. Highly stately, but the visual density diminishes legibility at small mobile and favicon resolutions.
